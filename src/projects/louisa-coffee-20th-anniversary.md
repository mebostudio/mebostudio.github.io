Over the past twenty years, the brand has continued to expand into international markets while exploring new and diverse dining experiences.\n\nFor its 20th anniversary, we developed a new key visual around the theme: “Dare to Venture, Stay True to Our Roots.” — “A stretch of dunes reaching toward the horizon, a journey twenty years in the making.”\n\nInspired by the shared colors, grains, and textures of desert sand and ground coffee, the two elements merge into a single landscape. Warm golden light carries forward Louisa’s familiar coffee tones, bringing together a spirit of adventure and a commitment to its roots within the same scene.\n\nAlways moving forward, while staying true to its passion for coffee. Twenty years, one cup of coffee. A journey forward, with the same passion at its heart."
