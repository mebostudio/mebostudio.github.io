overview: "A Visual Experiment for the Year of the Horse, 2026.\n\nThis work takes “Ponytail” as its core visual language, responding to the cultural symbolism of the Year of the Horse in 2026. Through digital generation and AI-assisted techniques, it reinterprets traditional zodiac imagery within a contemporary fashion context.\n\nIn the image, only the front half of the horse is revealed, with its tail deliberately omitted. The concept of the “tail” is instead displaced and extended into the female model’s hairstyle, creating a visual misalignment and connection between human and animal. This approach breaks linear storytelling by transforming an animal’s physical attribute into a human styling element, generating a visual tension that is both contrasting and fluid.\n\nBlending the visual language of fashion photography with digitally generated textures, the work explores how AI, as a creative tool, can move beyond realistic representation to become a medium for conceptual extension and visual storytelling."
